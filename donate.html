Where your donation goes

Servers, bandwidth and domain names. Donations also let us move to more powerful servers, add storage (hard drives) and grow the network. Online play itself stays free for everyone.

A thank-you on your profile

Any donation gives your Nextendo account the Donor badge, and the Donor role on our Discord server if your Discord account is linked. Both are added automatically, usually within a few minutes, and they stay for good.
