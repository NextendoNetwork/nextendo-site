          Frequently asked questions

          Is Nextendo affiliated with Nintendo?

          No. Nextendo is an independent community project, with no affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement from Nintendo Co., Ltd.

          Do I have to provide games?

          Nextendo doesn't distribute games, ROMs or keys. It only provides network services: you stay responsible for your own copies.

          Which emulators are supported?

          Ryujinx Nextendo and Citron Nextendo, plus a real Switch running custom firmware. More may follow as the community contributes.

          What do you do with my data?

          Only what the service needs to run. No tracking, no selling. The code is open, so you can check it.

          How can I contribute?

          Code, translations, compatibility testing or moderation: everything helps. Join the repository and the community server to start.

          Ready to reconnect your Switch?

          Follow the development, join the community and start your first online match.
