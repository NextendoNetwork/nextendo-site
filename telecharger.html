On a real Switch (CFW)

Nextendo also works on a Switch running custom firmware. Everything is reversible.

  1. Install the Nextendo profile
    It redirects the console's online services to Nextendo.
  2. Link your account
    Sign in from the console with your Nextendo e-mail and password.
  3. Play online
    Open any supported game's online mode.
